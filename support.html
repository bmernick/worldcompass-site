The needle looks wrong

Magnets and metal nearby disturb any compass: a MagSafe case or charger, a car, a laptop, a metal desk. If a red ! appears next to the accuracy, move your iPhone away. Your iPhone calibrates its compass by itself in a few seconds.

The app doesn't point to my destination

It needs your location. In iPhone Settings › World Compass › Location, choose "While Using the App".

I changed iPhones and lost PRO

In the app, open Settings (gear icon) and tap Restore purchase, using the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I use the widget?

With PRO, touch and hold your Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit or Customize and add World Compass. It shows the last destination you chose, with North up.
